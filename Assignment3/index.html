About NOSHAM Engineering Society

Our story, our mission, and why you should join us

March 5, 2026 NOSHAM Committee

Founded in 2020 by a passionate group of engineering students, NOSHAM has grown into one of the most active technical clubs at Monash University. Our society bridges the gap between academic theory and industry application, empowering students to become the engineers of tomorrow.

We believe that engineering and innovation go hand in hand. Our weekly sessions provide a hands-on, collaborative space to learn new tools, tackle real-world problems, and build connections that last a lifetime. Our committee is made up of dedicated volunteers who organise events, manage industry partnerships, and champion student-led projects.

We offer three core pillars to our members: Workshops — hands-on technical sessions covering CAD, 3D printing, and complex system design; Competitions — national and internal challenges designed to push the boundaries of student innovation; and Networking — direct pipelines to industry leaders and alumni currently shaping the tech world.
